Birdie Blasters

The golf clubs have been swapped for guns. You stand where your shot lands, so the next player can shoot you back toward the tee.

Players
  • The golf bag holds eight weapons, one for each club. The bazooka is your driver and the dart blaster is your putter. Every weapon has its own special effect.
  • The wind changes from hole to hole, and sand and rough take power from your shot.
  • You aim through your character’s eyes: with the mouse on a computer, by dragging on a phone. Hold to build power and release to fire.
  • Hit an opponent and they fly 40 m back. A headshot sends them all the way back to the tee.
  • Fewest strokes after three holes wins.
